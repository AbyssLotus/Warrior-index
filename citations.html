AI Citations

A log of AI tool use across this portfolio, in line with the course’s citation policy.

Course AI Policy, at a Glance

May Use Freely (No Citation)

  • Executive functions (time management, task breakdown)
  • Organizing notes from course documents
  • Live captioning course videos
  • Refining search queries
  • Voice control / listening to content
  • Grammar and spelling assistance
  • Feedback on writing or understanding

May Use With Citation

  • Generating ideas
  • Editing text beyond spelling/grammar
  • Generating code that is NOT the point of the assignment

Never Allowed (Even Cited)

  • Writing assignments
  • Generating code that IS the point of the assignment
  • Summarizing assigned reading

Citation is required only for the middle column. Uncited AI-generated or AI-assisted content is treated as an academic integrity violation; citing a “never allowed” use does not make it acceptable.

Format 1

Single-Use Citations
